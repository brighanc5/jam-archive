## Official recording-source links added

- **Ludlow Garage:** https://allmanbrothersband.com/discography/live-at-ludlow-garage-1970/ — CD release and track listing. **Important:** official concert index is April 4, 1970; other recording-release metadata has circulated an April 11 date. Our site discloses the uncertainty.
- **Atlanta Pop Festival, July 3 and July 5 billed dates:** https://allmanbrothersband.com/discography/the-allman-brothers-band-live-at-the-atlanta-intnl-pop-festival-july-3-5-1970/ — two separately indexed source pointers to the same multi-night album. The official discography describes the second performance as occurring around **3:50 a.m. July 6**, despite July 5 labeling.
- **Duane Allman's final show:** https://allmanbrothersband.com/discography/the-final-note-painters-mill-music-fair-owings-mills-md-10-17-71/ — the release identifies the source as Sam Idas's handheld **audience cassette**. Do not describe it as a soundboard recording.
- **Grateful Dead May 5, May 7, May 8 and May 9, 1977:** https://www.rhino.com/article/pre-order-grateful-dead-may-1977-get-shown-the-light-and-cornell-5877 — Rhino's official 11-disc release announcement names these four performances and Betty board/Plangent transfers. Four distinct concert-to-release pointers are indexed, each sharing a release URL. The Cornell dossier already had independently documented audience, soundboard-derived, and matrix tapes.

## Limits

These are **eight show-to-release references, not eight unique albums**. The site does not guarantee source availability or that the release metadata is entirely without dispute. Artist pages, concert dossiers and the Time Machine intentionally cite original source links rather than reproducing artwork, audio, or long third-party texts. New claims require independent source inspection and review, not an automatic scraper.
