# v0.14 — Show Studies / The Jam Archive after dark

## What's new

- 17 source-linked deep show studies covering five original Allman Brothers Fillmore sets, four Grateful Dead shows, four 1997 Phish shows, and four 2024 Goose shows.
- 238 individual source-listed song positions, 60 source-printed transition markers, three carefully scoped guest credits, 13 named source references. These are editorial excerpts, not automated audio analysis or verified complete setlists for the whole archive.
- A new repertoire directory covering 158 unique **literal song titles** across the 17 studies. Song-title matches do not establish composition identity, copyright, authorship, or teases.
- **Original psychedelic print identity:** dark aubergine backdrop; warm poster ink; vivid coral, amber, mint, teal and purple; a custom tie-dye motif built with CSS rather than copied band imagery; readable surfaces. No third-party fonts or imagery.
- **Mobile:** fixed five-destination lower navigation, single-column lists, 44+ px touch targets, and safe-area padding.
- **Long-list controls:** search results, Archive Atlas, Repertoire, Song Paths, Time Machine, Deep Archive and the recording-source directory now have incremental *Show more* controls. Filters operate on all records and reveal matches in batches; content remains readable without JavaScript.
- Simplified language on the major discovery pages. Specific source limitations still use precise language so the site does not accidentally misrepresent recordings or guests.

## Scope and verification

- Complete v0.13 source remains intact.
- Deterministic offline build; static hosting; no scheduled agent, hosted copyright audio, or paid API dependency.
- Tests verify page links, 17-study source references, scope, source URLs, original archive content, and addition of mobile/nav/assets to all generated HTML pages.
- **Visual caveat:** browser navigation was blocked by the development sandbox during final inspection. Automated static and JavaScript-syntax tests passed; a real device/browsing QA check is still required after deployment. The installation guide includes a manual phone checklist.

## Research standards

All song lists, reported segue markers, and guest credits refer to the cited source. An official album track list is not a master tape catalog. An indexed date-and-venue record is not evidence that an audience recording, full setlist, or guest recording exists. No concert recordings are hosted or embedded.
